FEATURED WORK

Projects

ROS 2 · AUTONOMOUS ROBOTS

Autonomous Trolley Localization and Docking

Developed an autonomous docking system for a Neobotix ROX-Diff mobile robot using UWB-based coarse localization and LiDAR-based geometric trolley detection.

  • Average docking time: 22.64 seconds
  • Mean lateral error: 0.26 cm
  • Mean orientation error: 0.003 rad
