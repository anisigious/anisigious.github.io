HUMAN-ROBOT INTERACTION · MACHINE VISION

Building a Curious Robot

This project involved transforming an ABB IRB 2600 industrial robotic arm into a human-interactive robot. The goal was to enable the robot to recognize human gestures and react accordingly.

The project consisted of two major parts. The first was the machine-vision system used to detect and recognize human gestures. The second involved sending specific instructions to the robot based on the recognized gesture, allowing it to perform predefined movements and responses.
